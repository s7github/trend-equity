# Implementation Roadmap: Research-Driven Growth for Trend-Equity

## Overview

This document provides a comprehensive, actionable implementation roadmap for establishing research-driven growth at Trend-Equity. It outlines specific activities, timelines, responsibilities, and success metrics for implementing the research strategy across the organization.

## Executive Summary

**Mission:** Establish research as the foundation of Trend-Equity's competitive advantage and sustainable growth
**Timeline:** 36-month implementation period (Months 1-36)
**Investment:** $15M total investment across implementation phases
**Expected Outcomes:** 3x revenue growth, 40% improvement in user engagement, establishment as market leader

## Implementation Framework

### Core Implementation Pillars:

1. **Research Infrastructure Foundation** (Months 1-12)
2. **Research Capability Development** (Months 1-24)
3. **Strategic Research Integration** (Months 6-36)
4. **Research Excellence & Innovation** (Months 18-36)
5. **Business Impact Measurement** (Months 1-36 - Ongoing)

## Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-12)

### Timeline & Objectives

**Months 1-3: Infrastructure Setup**

- Establish research governance and oversight structures
- Build core research team and capabilities
- Implement research documentation and knowledge management systems
- Set up research data collection and analysis platforms

**Months 4-9: Process Establishment**

- Define and implement research processes and standards
- Establish quality assurance and validation frameworks
- Develop research methodology guidelines
- Create research training and development programs

**Months 10-12: Initial Implementation**

- Launch initial research capabilities
- Establish baseline metrics and performance measurements
- Implement early research projects and initiatives
- Begin integration with product development processes
